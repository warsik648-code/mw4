Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare characters are the people in the campaign and the soldier you build in multiplayer. A Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare character search is that topic. It is not a roster of hero-shooter legends. Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare legend and the plural legends show up because other games use those words. This page says what this game actually has, including skin and set in the sense of camouflage and create-a-class.

## Campaign figures

The campaign follows the SAS and the United States Marine Corps. Names players still use are Captain Price, John “Soap” MacTavish, Gaz, Sergeant Paul Jackson, and Staff Sergeant Griggs, with antagonists such as Khaled Al-Asad and Imran Zakhaev. Those are characters in a story. They are not operators you unlock in a store, and they are not a tier list. You do not equip “Price” as a multiplayer legend. If a guide ranks them like a hero roster, it imported a different genre. The [rank guide](/ranks-tier-list/) makes the same point from the progression side.

## Multiplayer is a set you build

Multiplayer is create-a-class. You pick weapons, attachments as the game offered them, perks, and a killstreak path. That loadout is the closest thing to a “set”. A skin, for this game, is camouflage: the patterns you saw on weapons, including the gold camouflage players associated with completing weapon challenges, not a rotating shop of outfits. Newer Call of Duty games taught people to expect a cosmetic store. Searching Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare skin with that expectation will not find a battle-pass catalog on this site, and we will not invent one.

A character here can also mean the multiplayer body you see in a match. It is still not a named legend with a pick rate. Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare pick rates, if you want that discussion without fake percentages, stay on the rank page.

## What the product does not unlock

Loot ESP, on the [product page](/modern-warfare-cheats/), is an item-visual feature name. It does not grant a skin, a character, or a campaign model. Player ESP does not turn your soldier into a different character. There is no “unlock all characters” plan because that is not how this game’s multiplayer is structured. The [buyer guide](/cheats-guide/) keeps the commercial list separate from this vocabulary on purpose.

If you came from a skin-changer page about another game, you are reading a retargeted site. The old cosmetic-preview product is not part of this listing. Cloud DMA and HWID Spoofer, on the [store](/store/), are related products with their own prices. They are not character packs.

## Play, and the maps those characters stand on

To play is to run the client: campaign missions or a multiplayer match on a map such as Crash, Vacant, or Overgrown. The [map guide](/map-rotation/) names the layouts. The [release guide](/release-date/) places the whole cast in 2007, not in a 2026 season. A new skin for 2026 is not a claim this site will make. A new map for 2026 is the same kind of claim, and it is declined in the map article.

## Names to keep straight

Use character for Price, Soap, Gaz, and the multiplayer soldier. Use legend only when you are explaining that this game does not use that system. Use skin for camouflage, not for a shop bundle. Use set for a create-a-class loadout. Use those words on purpose and the searches still land on a page that tells the truth.

The Windows PC product remains optional reading. Its plans are $35 and $150. Its feature names are unchanged by this article. If a character question was really a purchase question, the [FAQ](/faq/) is shorter. If it was really “who is in the campaign”, the names above are the answer, and they do not require a checkout.


## Campaign missions are not a class menu

The campaign is a sequence of missions with scripted characters. You do not pick Price or Soap from a grid before a chapter. Multiplayer is the opposite: you build a soldier who has no campaign name. Keeping that split stops a skin search from turning into a request for “all characters”. Gold camouflage, where players remember it, was tied to weapon challenges inside multiplayer, not to buying a bundle on this website. A set of perks that you like is still a create-a-class, and you can write it down without any product.

If a video titles itself with legends, look at the roster on screen. Named abilities and a pick-a-hero screen mean a different game. Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare will show weapons, perks, and killstreaks. That visual check is faster than trusting the title. The same check applies to a “new skin 2026” thumbnail. If the interface is a modern cosmetic shop, you are not looking at the 2007 camouflage system this article describes.

Nothing in that test changes the Windows PC product. Read it only after you know whether you wanted a character explanation or a checkout. The store remains available, and it still does not sell a character.

Mission briefings name the squad you follow. Multiplayer lobbies do not. If a friend asks for a character tier list, ask whether they mean the campaign cast or a weapon. The answer is different, and only the weapon question belongs anywhere near a tier conversation on the ranks page. Price and Soap are not pick rates. They are people in a 2007 story, and that is enough to answer the character search without a checkout.
