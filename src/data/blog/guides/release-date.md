The question “when did Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare come out” has a historical answer. Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare release date for North America was November 5, 2007. European releases followed within days. Infinity Ward developed the game and Activision published it. It shipped on Windows PC, Xbox 360, and PlayStation 3. That is the game this site’s product name refers to. Later games that reuse the words Modern Warfare are not the same release, even when a search box blends them.

## Street dates and the versions people mix up

North America saw the game on November 5, 2007. Other regions followed on their own dates, so “the” release date is the North American one unless you say otherwise. A remaster, a later Modern Warfare, and a phone game with a similar name are not that Tuesday. If a download page shows a 2019-or-later system requirement, you are not holding the 2007 installer. The shader article is the warning for that mix-up. The player-count article is the warning against quoting a chart for the wrong build.

Collectors still talk about the original PC build, map packs, and how matchmaking aged. Those are history topics. They do not create a 2026 season, a new map, or a mobile port on this domain. When a search for “when did it come out” is actually “can I still play”, answer it with the client you can launch, not with a year alone. The year tells you which rules and which maps you should expect. The client tells you whether they are still in front of you.

The product listing is optional and separate. Its age is not 2007. Its prices are the ones on the store today. Do not let a release-date paragraph rewrite them.

If you only remember “Modern Warfare” and not the year, look at the main menu art and the create-a-class screen. The 2007 game does not look like a live-service lobby from the 2020s. That glance prevents a download of the wrong title, which is the practical reason the street date is worth knowing.
