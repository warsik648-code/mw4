## Player visuals

Player ESP is the general player-visual name. Weapon ESP, Health Bar, Distance ESP, Box ESP, and Skeleton ESP are the more specific names on the same list. A Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare ESP search and a wallhack search usually want this group. Readers also write Modern Warfare 4 ESP or Modern Warfare 4 wallhack for that same group. Wallhack is the older word for seeing player information through cover. This site uses ESP in the product copy and mentions wallhack here so the search and the feature list can be read together.

Loot ESP is the item-visual name. It is not a skin unlock and it is not a camouflage unlock. Cosmetic questions belong in the [characters and skins](/characters-skins/) guide. Loot, in a 2007 multiplayer match, is not a battle-royale pickup system. The feature name is still the one printed on the product, and it should not be stretched into a claim about a mode this game did not ship with.

## Radar, in the game and on the product

Call of Duty 4 multiplayer already has a UAV killstreak. That is an in-game radar reward, not a third-party feature. When this site says radar, it means the radar wording used next to the ESP features in the [preview](/preview/). MW4 radar hack is the search phrase for that wording. The in-game UAV and a product radar term are easy to mix up. They are not the same control, and buying the product does not add a UAV to your create-a-class.
